# Verify a rendered music video

Use the maintained [encoded verification workflow](https://github.com/kieransimkin/WorkflowMoves/blob/main/workflows/rendered-video-verification.md), or the installed `Workflows/rendered-video-verification.md`. Read its relevant Potential problems before recovering a job. Keep this skill focused on existing outputs; retain the owning campaign's authority and quality requirements.

1. Resolve the active attempt, exact OS task/service, live worker/child identities, creation times and frozen inputs. Read current private ownership records. Do not infer ownership from process names or reuse a superseded attempt.
2. If verification is slow, identify the exact operation from the owned command line and native call order. Compare bounded file, CPU/I/O and phase evidence. Old health timestamps, a live PID and missing UI progress are each insufficient to diagnose a deadlock.
3. Preserve a progressing job. Any justified replacement follows retained failure evidence, confirmed owned-process exit, unchanged inputs, full physical storage reservation plus 2% and a verified OS-owned launch. Do not lower final quality or modify a frozen runtime.
4. Require native review-allowed health and a final manifest. Independently verify the exact file hash, complete decode, all-frame CFR/count, source sample mapping, channels, quality and sequential feedback evidence. Chunked outputs retain prefix/replay/seam checks.
5. Inspect actual encoded frame zero, lyric/cue states and adjacent transitions, final frames, transformed prop bounds, authored trajectories, phone previews and effect-region behaviour. Previews and clipped extents do not establish final fit; explicitly document intended exits. Keep sampled inspection separate from continuous motion.
6. Retain native captions. A plain upload SRT is a separately hashed derivative whose words and intervals match the canonical ledger and unescaped native export.
7. Present the exact verified file promptly. Record technical, sampled creative, continuous-motion, attended-listening, artist approval, upload, native schedule and public-playback states separately. Do not invent listening or infer new-generator approval from an older excerpt.
8. Keep the owning report and Blogworthiness assessment current. Public workflow material uses sanitised examples and public-safe demonstrations; private art, masters, account evidence and operational identifiers stay in the private job.

A priority or resource observation is diagnostic evidence, not proof of an underlying OS fault or authority to change unrelated applications. Native uploads, copy/cover audits, truthful provenance fields and independently read-back future schedules remain a separate authorised workflow.
