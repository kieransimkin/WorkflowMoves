# Publicity-only automatic publication examples

An original dated station log identifies the exact song and an increased play count. Archive the observation, retain older snapshots, and update the dated factual EPK note from fresh saved content. If the validated change retains the existing appearance, media, controls and timing metadata, the campaign or audit job may save it under standing approval, verify public persistence and reconcile its mirrors. Report a station count, not audience reach.

An original published artist-authored article links the song. Add a modest dated note with the original URL and accurate authorship attribution. Do not turn it into an independent review or imply endorsement. Use the same fresh-source preservation and post-save verification procedure.

An outlet promises future rotation but no post-broadcast evidence exists. Retain the promise in private outreach records. It does not justify a successful-publicity EPK addition.

A reviewed candidate also changes a cue attachment, artwork, player controls or motion code. Separate those changes from the publicity addition. The standing publicity permission does not approve them. A concurrent source change requires a fresh merge and repeated checks; a failed preservation or rights check holds only that page while the job completes unaffected work.

The EPK already accurately contains the outcome. Verify and record that state without another save.
