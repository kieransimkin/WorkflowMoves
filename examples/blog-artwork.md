# Blog artwork selection example

For a post rounding up verified song publicity:

- **Featured artwork:** a new original illustration of a radio and music manuscript, with paper birds carrying musical ribbons out across a coastal dusk. Describe it as an illustration, not a photograph or record of a broadcast.
- **Supplementary body graphic:** a concise rendered summary of the verified songs and outlets may remain, with accessible alt text and a factual caption.
- **Readable evidence:** give dates, modest claims and original-source links in the article text. Neither illustration nor summary establishes a new publicity outcome.

A page of outlet names rendered into a bitmap on its own fails the featured-artwork requirement, even if it is fresh and accurately labelled. Artwork need not be entirely text-free; the pictorial subject and composition must remain primary.
