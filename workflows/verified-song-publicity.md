# Immediate verified-song publicity reconciliation

Run as soon as primary evidence establishes a public song feature, including ended campaigns and future releases. Research, archive and prepare automatically; exact public-save approval remains necessary.

1. Resolve the exact song and recording. Read its current plan, keyword policy, publicity ledger and original public item or authoritative post-broadcast evidence. Classify authored contribution, independent coverage, airplay, playlist placement or public discussion accurately. Exclude ordinary release availability, owned promotion, private compliments, invitations, receipts and promises from earned-publicity claims.
2. Preserve permissible primary evidence privately under `Releases/<song>/evidence/publicity-airplay-discussion/<date>-<source>/`. Record original URL/private reference, event/publication date and timezone if known, verification date/method, supported claim, uncertainties, public treatment and evidence hashes. Follow contextual broadcast and reuse rules in `Workflows/VERIFIED-COVERAGE-EPK-ARCHIVE.md`.
3. Reconcile the release-local ledger. Deduplicate by song/source/event; update cumulative station-log counts with an observation date while preserving previous snapshots. Counts are station-reported evidence, not listener reach. Unknown publication dates remain unknown; first verification is not a fabricated publication date. Correct disproven claims transparently.
4. Inspect fresh saved editor content and the rendered EPK. Preserve canonical design, lyrics, players, metadata timing, controls, downloads, accessibility and existing publicity. Compare every verified eligible instance with the page. Already accurate entries need verification only.
5. Stage a narrow additive semantic section or refresh an existing entry with dated, modest text and direct original links. Identify artist-authored guest articles accurately. Do not publish correspondence, personal contacts, logos, screenshots, recordings or rights documents without the necessary permission. Plain factual linking may be the appropriate treatment.
6. Use the WordPress EPK publisher skill to validate the exact candidate: UTF-8, retained features, desktop/tablet/mobile layout, accessible links, duplicates and timing-source authority. Record source/candidate hashes and previews. Preserve supported exact track/timing association and approved choreography; unresolved source mapping remains a hold.
7. Obtain existing exact-payload action-time confirmation for the page, candidate and any associated property change. Save once, reload persisted editor content, then verify independent signed-out responsive output and source links. Record revision, hashes and limits. A save notice or authenticated preview alone does not prove the public result.
8. Reconcile durable release-local mirrors/generator inputs, ledger and same-date campaign/report. Finish all unaffected work before handing back an approval or verified blocker. Never defer the EPK step merely because the audit itself is complete.

Minimum ledger fields: stable item ID; outlet/venue; exact song/recording; result type; event/publication date (or unknown); original URL/private evidence reference; verification date/method; exact supported claim; public-display permission; EPK URL; locally recorded/staged/approved/published/verified-live states; candidate/revision identity; correction/history notes.

`Tools/publicity_inventory.py` is a read-only candidate finder for local marketing text: `python Tools/publicity_inventory.py Releases --output <private-report>/inventory.json`. It hashes reviewed files and reports skipped/unavailable evidence. Every match remains `candidate_detected`; lexical matching never verifies publicity. Review primary evidence manually. Keep inventories and private evidence outside GitHub packages.

Campaign runs, email-reply audits and weekly syncs must reconcile new evidence, duplicates and pending EPK publication. An installed rule or future automation proves only workflow configuration. The ledger and signed-out page establish whether an outcome was recorded and published.
