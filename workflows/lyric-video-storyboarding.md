# Storyboard lyric videos and Shorts

Use the maintained [storyboarding skill](https://github.com/kieransimkin/WorkflowMoves/blob/main/skills/storyboard-lyric-videos/SKILL.md), installed as `storyboard-lyric-videos`, before choosing scenes for a lyric video or music Short. It compares lyric-led narrative, a single visual/effect hook and justified alternatives using the actual campaign, audio, visual and audience evidence.

![A lyric-led journey and a single changing window offer different visual treatments](media/storyboard-treatment-comparison.svg)

*Generated schematic planning illustration with an invented premise. It is not rendered song footage, an artist biography or audience evidence.*

**Inputs:** the current release plan and creative approvals; canonical master, lyrics and timing; saved StemLab and visual-language analysis; approved campaign assets; destination constraints; available native audience evidence.

**Outputs:** contrasting treatments and a reasoned choice; source-bound shot/state timing; opening/development/payoff contact sheets; a production/asset brief; a scoped test plan and an immediate skill retrospective. Use the skill's [storyboard template](https://github.com/kieransimkin/WorkflowMoves/blob/main/skills/storyboard-lyric-videos/assets/storyboard-template.md) and [evidence guide](https://github.com/kieransimkin/WorkflowMoves/blob/main/skills/storyboard-lyric-videos/references/evidence-and-learning.md).

**Acceptance:** every selected interval and meaningful lyric/music change is accounted for; source and output clocks stay distinct; the hook develops and pays off; lyric meaning, campaign identity, text and destination crops remain reviewable; audience claims match their evidence. Each run records what could improve the skill itself, applies a justified authorised correction or retains its exact blocker, and records why no change is needed when appropriate. Finish with the project's blogworthiness assessment and evidence.

**Limits:** a proposal, private preference review, ad study, model score or public view count does not prove audience capture. Missing analytics permit a provisional treatment with a falsifiable measurement plan. Storyboarding does not authorise a render, spend, model download or publication.

Preview `python install.py --root WORKSPACE --routes lyric-video-storyboarding-routes.json --check`, inspect the focused changes, apply that same manifest, and require the repeat preview to report current files. It installs the complete skill in the project source and discoverable skill directory, plus this guide and illustration. It preserves provider-managed skills and existing campaign records.

Use [lyric timing review](lyric-timing-review.md) for unresolved sung boundaries and [FrameRudiments feature selection](framerudiments-rendering-features.md) when handing a treatment to production. FrameRudiments owns offline composition; StemLab owns audio and timing; DanceRudiments native movement; DanceMoves live web motion.

## Potential problems

If source identities conflict, keep precise timing pending and retain the conceptual brief. If audience evidence is missing, label the creative hypothesis and plan a later native readback. If the chosen hook cannot be judged from stills, use an authorised bounded animatic, preserving its original source clock; a contact sheet cannot verify continuous motion. See the skill's Potential problems for recovery and installation holds.
