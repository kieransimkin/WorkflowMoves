# Audience evidence and learning

Read when choosing a treatment, designing an audience test or reviewing results. Refresh changeable platform definitions and guidance at use time through supported connectors or primary sources. The sources below were checked on 24 November 2026; they are starting references, not permanent rules or proof about this campaign.

## What different evidence can establish

| Evidence | Valid use | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This artist's native analytics and retained exact-asset tests | Locate capture/retention problems and compare relevant treatments against a channel-specific baseline. | Distribution, audience, age, duration, traffic source, packaging and promotion can confound differences. Observational comparisons do not establish causation. |
| Current official platform measurement documentation | Define the metric, its denominator, eligible surface and processing limits. | A metric definition is not creative-effectiveness research. Missing fields remain unavailable. |
| Primary research or platform studies with disclosed methods | Form a scoped hypothesis; inspect sample, date, population, exposure, outcome, controls and effect uncertainty. | Ad recall, purchase intent or aggregate ads findings cannot be transferred into organic music retention claims. Note missing methods. |
| Official creator interviews and comparable public videos | Discover possible mechanisms and inspect their actual opening, development and ending. | Successful creators and high-view examples are selected cases. Their advice and public counts do not prove a hook caused success. |
| Model analysis, editorial judgement and a private concept review | Generate and improve a plausible treatment, inspect meaning, legibility and feasibility. | These do not measure audience capture, retention, listening, safety or rights clearance. |

For each material recommendation retain evidence ID, source URL/path or native video ID, access date, exact asset/variant, format/platform, observation window/video age, audience/traffic source, duration, paid/organic status, metric definition/units/denominator and availability. Include confidence and scope limits, the proposed creative mechanism, the concrete storyboard decision and how it could be disproved. Keep private reports private; publish only cleared summaries.

Inspect comparable examples across outcomes when such evidence exists, not just winners. Match song type, format, duration, audience size, traffic source and publication age as far as practical. Group crops/reuploads as one lineage. Describe first-frame subject/action, visual question, timing of change, text load and payoff rather than copying a recognisable character, composition or sequence. Do not claim completeness from a small sample.

## Verified primary starting sources

### YouTube: Shorts storytelling

[YouTube's January 2025 conversation with Todd Sherman and Jenny Hoyos](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/) discusses an immediately engaging opening, compact stories and brief moments. It supports exploring both narrative and single-moment treatments. The opening-speed advice is a creator's experience in an official interview, not a controlled finding or universal one-second deadline. Treat it as a hypothesis to adapt to the song; it does not require shock, misleading suspense or faster cuts.

### YouTube: measuring capture and watch depth

[Content performance definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en) distinguish initial staying behaviour, engaged views and watch depth. Use the current native definitions and exposure base in the retained report; definitions can change. A play count alone cannot show whether a storyboard captured attention, and metrics conditioned on viewers who stayed should not be interpreted as describing everyone exposed. Keep thumbnail-led discovery and Shorts-feed capture separate.

[Key moments for audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en) provides time-resolved review of holds, declines, spikes and dips. A spike can reflect revisiting a confusing passage as well as interest. Inspect the actual scene and other evidence before assigning a cause. Report availability and processing limits explicitly; do not promise highlighted moments on every Short or invent missing graph points.

### TikTok: advertising creative guidance

[TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes) describes a hook, development and close, plus platform-conscious framing, movement and sound. Use this to motivate testing an early, understandable visual promise and an earned resolution. Its advertising context does not establish an organic-music benchmark. Preserve the song's soundtrack, lyric pacing and audience purpose instead of importing a product-ad call to action or a fixed stimulation quota.

For Instagram/Reels or other destinations, inspect their current native analytics and official guidance separately when relevant. Do not relabel YouTube or TikTok evidence as proof for another platform. Existing vidIQ or native connector access may help with authorised read-only analysis; missing access should narrow confidence, not trigger account changes or paid research.

## A small, useful test plan

1. **State the decision.** For example: does a transforming object capture more of this audience than a lyric-led journey while preserving comprehension and listening intent? This is a question, not an expected winner.
2. **Separate two tests.** A whole-treatment comparison changes many things and can inform creative choice. A diagnostic test isolates one change, such as opening action, while holding audio excerpt, duration, text, crop and production quality constant where practical. Do not attribute the former's outcome to one component.
3. **Write the prediction before seeing results.** Record the intended mechanism, variant IDs/hashes, primary metric, baseline/control, guardrails and the evidence that would change the decision. Keep quality/meaning and downstream listening alongside capture.
4. **Choose the native metric for the surface.** For Shorts-feed work use available initial-stay/swipe measures for capture, then relevant retention/completion or watch depth for sustain. For a full lyric video use its actual discovery surface and early/section retention; thumbnail response and inside-video retention answer different questions. On other platforms retain their own definitions and denominators. A review panel can assess comprehension but supplies no native audience metric.
5. **Bound the observation.** Set a comparable publication-age window, processing allowance and minimum relevant exposure before launch, using the account's available baseline and practical sensitivity. Report actual numerators/denominators where available and uncertainty. No universal view threshold, retention percentage or posting-hour rule belongs in this skill. An underexposed, unsettled or confounded comparison is inconclusive.
6. **Read the result at scene times.** Map dips/spikes/holds to the exact output timestamps and source lyric/cue. Examine plausible alternatives such as unreadable words, a slow setup, audience mismatch or confusing payoff. Loop-related watch percentages do not prove unique people replayed deliberately. Avoid stopping on the first favourable fluctuation or comparing unlike traffic mixes.
7. **Keep the conclusion proportionate.** Prefer another matched case or replication before generalising. Record evidence for, against and unknown. Assess listening using actually observed native listening or qualified attribution; clicks, views and posting receipts do not establish streams. No tests are published or scheduled without their existing authority.

## Retrospective and skill change receipt

Every run, including planning-only and failed runs, records:

- run ID/date, owning release/report, skill source revision/hash and treatment chosen;
- the evidence actually available, observations from concept/preview review and any later audience readback;
- what helped, what failed or wasted effort, unresolved uncertainty and a candidate improvement to the skill;
- the decision: applied correction, no justified change with reason, or retained proposal with exact blocker/owner;
- changed canonical paths, public-safe rationale, validation/contrasting-case result and source/install/release state when a patch was made;
- the next evidence review and owner, plus the required blogworthiness decision, reason and evidence.

Evaluate the process immediately. Do not wait for an upload or analytics to notice that the template omitted output time or encouraged an unsupported claim. Equally, do not turn private campaign preferences, inferred performance or one successful video into a general mandate. Keep hypotheses and disconfirming evidence so improvements can be reconsidered. The retrospective records a finding; only a validated, read-back change proves the skill was improved.
