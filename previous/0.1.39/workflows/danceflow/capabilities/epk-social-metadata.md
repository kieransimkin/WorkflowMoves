Inspected stable source: [v1.2.5](https://github.com/kieransimkin/kieran-epk-social-metadata/releases/tag/v1.2.5), commit `9e1470e7431629a15302773b04bfc622307d0f90`. This is a dated baseline; [check current releases before every new workflow](../workflows/release-preflight.md).

## Choose and use a route

| Goal | Supported route and evidence |
|---|---|
| Store verified facts | Use canonical music/page/audio identity, descriptions, artwork dimensions, descriptive phrases and guarded imports. WordPress retains title/canonical/robots/sitemap ownership. |
| Describe public video | Verify exact public video, original upload date, duration, thumbnail, playback and allowed player/VideoObject controls. Unknown facts remain absent. |
| Verify deployment | Inspect saved metadata and anonymous source, avoid duplicate owners and preserve page media/timing. Correct markup does not establish ranking, a search thumbnail or social card delivery. |
