## Keep published campaign media in its EPK

Standing instruction from Kieran, 12 October 2026: whenever a project chat, agent, campaign or scheduled workflow publishes a campaign video, YouTube Short or social post, reconcile the same material into every relevant campaign EPK during that same job. Apply to past and future releases, recovering earlier omissions in campaign reviews and catalogue audits. Follow Workflows/campaign-media-epk-sync.md.

- Embed each distinct published YouTube video and Short wherever YouTube permits. For the same video published across several platforms, show one EPK item using its YouTube embed. Retain cross-platform receipts privately. Group only proven identical assets using exact render hashes or verified cross-post mappings; do not merge distinct edits, Shorts, performances or versions by similar titles.
- Verify exact campaign association, original publication and public availability. Keep publication, playback, embedding, persisted scheduling, drafts and local renders distinct. Reconcile scheduled items after verified publication; never expose embargoed, private or unlisted review material.
- Preserve the approved canonical full video in its existing hero. Add genuinely different Shorts and other posts cumulatively in a release-specific, accessible campaign-media section. Distinct text, image and carousel posts may use permissible same-site material, supported embeds or modest dated descriptions with direct original links. Artist promotion is not independent coverage or endorsement.
- Use titled, responsive YouTube iframes without autoplay and with a suitable portrait layout for Shorts. Preserve controls, fullscreen, keyboard access and privacy/consent behaviour. When YouTube embedding is unavailable, use the verified original campaign render: upload or reuse its exact bytes in WordPress and show one native, user-controlled HTML video player with its approved poster. Verify local/hosted hashes, attachment identity and actual playback. Keep YouTube restrictions unchanged; never obtain a file by bypassing access controls. Do not substitute another edit or publish an uncleared/private source. If the original or hosting is unavailable, retain a direct link and precise hold. A loaded iframe or upload receipt does not prove playback.
- Standing instruction from Kieran, 15 October 2026: omit redundant "Watch on YouTube" links beneath embedded videos and Shorts. Preserve each EPK's existing styled YouTube buttons, meaningful captions, source citations and link-only fallbacks for unavailable embeds. Remove only the matching embedded player's redundant watch anchor; keep all player, artwork, effect and timing source unchanged. Use Tools/EPK Campaign Media/clean_epk_video_links.py for exact-span preparation and retain its evidence.
- Merge from fresh saved editor content, preserving history, media, design, controls, lyrics, downloads, timing metadata, choreography and mobile sensor bindings. Validate exact candidate hashes, Unicode, responsive rendering, playback and retained features. Re-read before saving to detect another owner's changes. Keep the existing exact-payload action-time public-save gate; only independently eligible publicity-only additions may use the limited standing publicity permission. This rule does not grant blanket public saves, uploads, redesigns, rights commitments or plugin deployments.
- Keep release-local campaign-media evidence with asset/group identity, platform IDs/URLs, publication and verification dates, public-use eligibility, EPK targets, candidate/saved hashes and precise recorded locally, staged, awaiting approval, published and verified-live states. Reconcile mirrors/generator inputs after verified publication. Passing EPKs need no repeat save. Complete unaffected work and keep necessary EPK actions outstanding until approval or a verified blocker is resolved.

The hero video must contain the exact release master soundtrack. Connect every verified full-master player to the same DanceMoves playback source so the existing effects, lyrics and cues follow the active player. Map verified partial-track Shorts with explicit song offset, soundtrack duration and any player preroll; verify the original upload file and render/trim evidence. Never infer a mapping from a title or duration alone. Preserve alternate-track associations and report missing provenance as a precise hold. Use the shared registerYouTube/registerPlayer APIs and metadata-derived timing sources; page callbacks describe only the existing appearance.
