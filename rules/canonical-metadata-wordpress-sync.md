## Synchronize canonical song metadata into WordPress

Standing instruction from Kieran, 13 October 2026: whenever verified canonical BPM, lyric timing, cue timing or other critical song metadata changes, update every affected WordPress EPK during the same job. Apply to every project chat, release workflow and recurring audit. Saving a local canonical record alone does not complete the change.

- Resolve exact release, track and master identity from artist-approved authority and verified distributor records. Critical metadata includes BPM, canonical lyric wording and timing, cue timing, title/artist credits, official cover and public audio identity, release date/state, UPC/ISRC and verified platform links. Keep private masters, personal data, rights and permission evidence private. Do not infer a new authority from a filename, estimate or fallback.
- Capture fresh authenticated editor fields, selected attachment identities and canonical live presentation. Stage the smallest correction: BPM/metadata fields only where sufficient, exact canonical timing uploads/selections when required, or a narrowly merged visible-lyrics/fact/link change. Reconcile My Songs, calendars and social metadata when the changed fact affects them. Preserve all unrelated design, media, controls, mobile input, choreography and track associations.
- WordPress EPK Timing metadata remains the only timing-file authority. Players, renderers, cues and downloads consume its plugin-generated configuration; never insert timing URLs or a page-local source override. Validate UTF-8, exact hosted byte size/SHA-256, parser/event bounds and recording identity before selecting a file. Retain original attachments and backups; do not delete media or retime the recording to fit a BPM change.
- Complete the authorized WordPress synchronization, not merely an audit or local update. Honour mandatory tool/action-time approval gates; when one genuinely applies, present one exact narrow correction batch and finish unaffected work. Reuse already granted approval for unchanged exact targets/values/payloads; do not invent another general confirmation. Record a verified access, ownership, missing-source or approval blocker precisely and retain the correction for resumption.
- After saving, reload the editor and independently verify persisted values, anonymous public configuration, every dependent consumer, exact selected hosted bytes, parser state, retained features and representative responsive layouts. Keep property persistence, runtime loading, structural checks, synthetic motion and physical-phone evidence distinct. Restore temporary QA settings.
- Reconcile canonical records, fact sheets, asset manifests, attachment maps, current-source-authority, local mirrors and generator inputs with the verified WordPress result; update the existing dated reports and current audit states. A previously unknown BPM that an artist confirms becomes a known correction, not an unresolved source hold. Passing pages need no repeat save.
