## Automatically publish verified publicity additions

Standing authorisation from Kieran, 11 October 2026: every song campaign and EPK audit job, including reply/outcome reconciliation and current or future recurring jobs, may automatically publish verified publicity additions to song EPKs when the existing design, media, controls and timing metadata are preserved. Apply to past and future releases. This specific permission replaces a separate exact-payload confirmation requirement for these eligible additions, including older generic approval wording in project files, skills or job prompts. Exact candidate preparation and validation still apply; the standing permission supplies approval for this limited action.

- Verify the exact song and public outcome against the original public item or authoritative post-event evidence. Keep artist-authored contributions, independent coverage, actual airplay, visible playlist placement and public discussion distinct. Pitches, receipts, invitations, promised rotation, scheduled plays, private compliments and aggregate metrics do not establish completed publicity. Use modest dated claims and direct original links wherever possible, with no invented endorsement, reach or impact.
- Confirm the intended note is public-safe and permissible. Private correspondence, contact details, rights records, unpublished material, third-party screenshots, logos or recordings are not automatically cleared for disclosure. Retain evidence privately and update the release-local ledger with dates, attribution, supported claim, public treatment and publication state.
- Back up fresh saved editor content and rendered output. Record the source hash and exact selected lyric/cue metadata. Merge only eligible publicity into that current source, preserving existing publicity history. Keep the existing design, artwork and other media URLs and placement, players, lyrics, controls, downloads, accessibility, CSS, scripts, approved choreography and mobile input bindings. Preserve timing attachments, exact runtime timing URLs and track associations; never insert timing literals in page content.
- Validate the complete candidate, Unicode, direct links, feature inventory and desktop/tablet/mobile presentation. Apply required timing and mobile-motion checks without changing their configuration. Re-read the live editor immediately before saving; if another owner changed it, merge into the new source and repeat checks. Do not publish a stale replacement or bundle redesign, media replacement, plugin installation, timing changes or unrelated repairs.
- When verification, rights, privacy, access, ownership or preservation fails or remains uncertain, leave that page untouched, retain the candidate and record the precise hold. Complete unaffected work. Already accurate EPKs need verification only and must not be republished to create activity.
- Once all checks pass, save the publicity-only candidate automatically. Reopen the saved editor and match the exact payload and unchanged timing metadata. Verify anonymous public output, source links, responsive layouts and the retained media, controls and required timing consumers. Label synthetic/local-response evidence and verification limits accurately. A save notice alone is not proof of the public result.
- Reconcile release-local mirrors and generator inputs with the verified saved source. Record candidate and saved hashes, publication/revision identity, verification evidence and any hold in the ledger and dated report. Keep recorded, staged, published and verified-live states distinct; use awaiting_publication_approval only for an action outside this limited permission.

Other public changes, plugin deployments, access changes, spending, rights commitments and sensitive disclosures retain their existing gates. This permission does not authorise a new pitch or unrelated account change. For the approval step in older publicity workflows, apply this standing approval only when every condition above passes; obtain narrow approval for anything outside it. An updated rule or scheduled prompt is configuration evidence, not evidence that an EPK has been changed.
