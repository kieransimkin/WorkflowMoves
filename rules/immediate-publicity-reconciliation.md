## Reconcile verified song publicity immediately

Standing instruction from Kieran, 11 October 2026: as soon as any project chat, agent, campaign or scheduled workflow verifies publicity for a song, complete its release-local evidence record and prepare the cumulative EPK addition during that same job. Apply to past and future releases. Do not defer this work to the next campaign or weekly audit.

Use `Workflows/verified-song-publicity.md` and `Workflows/VERIFIED-COVERAGE-EPK-ARCHIVE.md`. Deduplicate by exact song, original outlet/source and event; retain dates, the supported claim, verification evidence, public-display permission and EPK state. Authored guest articles, independent coverage, station-reported plays, visible playlist placement and public discussion must retain distinct attribution. A pitch, receipt, invitation, promised rotation, private compliment, Listened status or aggregate playlist-add count is not a verified public feature.

Automatically archive permissible primary evidence, update the ledger, inspect the current live EPK and stage any missing eligible note from fresh editor content. Preserve existing publicity history and page features. Reconcile durable mirrors/generator inputs after publication. If the exact outcome is already accurately represented, verify and record it without republishing.

Public saves retain the existing exact-payload action-time confirmation gate. Present the reviewed target, text, source links, candidate hash and any required metadata change for narrow approval; after approval, save and verify persisted signed-out responsive output in the same job. Separate `recorded_locally`, `awaiting_publication_approval`, `published` and `verified_live`. At a necessary rights, access or ownership hold, retain the reviewed candidate and name the smallest remaining action. Never treat a saved rule or future schedule as evidence that an EPK was updated.

Every campaign run, reply audit and weekly release/EPK sync must reconcile new outcome evidence against the release-local ledger and live EPK. Record no verified outcome when appropriate; never add invented praise or pending submissions to the public page.
