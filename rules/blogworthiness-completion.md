## Mandatory blogworthiness check for every task and automation

Kieran, 21 October 2026: explicitly assess blogworthiness before completing every task, chat activity, agent assignment and automation run, including work outside My Songs. Keep this requirement near the start of instruction files and in every saved automation prompt so it does not depend on a later, truncated instruction block.

- Record `Blogworthiness: PASS` or `Blogworthiness: FAIL`, a short evidence-based reason and the source evidence in the existing task report or final handoff. Small tasks may use one sentence. A missing assessment is an incomplete handoff; a saved rule or prompt does not prove a running task applied it.
- PASS means enough verified, public-safe substance for a coherent story with a specific reader-interest angle: creative or musical progress; a useful technical feature, experiment, comparison, discovery, fix, failure or recovery; illuminating work in progress; a campaign milestone or other meaningful public development. Workflow improvement and publicity are possible angles, not prerequisites. Check current posts and pending drafts to prevent duplicate coverage.
- FAIL means trivial administration, substantial duplication, insufficient verified substance, no reader-interest angle, or privacy constraints that remove the point of the story. Do not create a post solely about applying this check, changing blogging rules or completing publication administration.
- Keep editorial merit separate from delivery. Missing artwork, access or a publication route is a named blocker on a passing story, not a reason to relabel it FAIL. For every PASS, record the angle, evidence, duplicate check and next action: covered by a linked existing post, prepared/draft, scheduled, published, verified live, or blocked with the smallest specific requirement and a named continuation. Do not let bundling postpone a ready story indefinitely.
- Use the maintained project-blog-writer skill and Kieran's voice guidance for qualifying articles. Preserve fresh pictorial featured artwork, relevant in-body rich media, actual demonstrations of dynamic features, source attribution, privacy, rights, publication timing and independent public verification. Never infer permission to disclose private records or publish unsafe material from a passing assessment.
- Add this explicit completion requirement when creating or updating an automation. Preserve its original purpose, schedule, paused/active state, destination owner, notification policy, stop rules, costs and other approvals. A quiet/no-op or read-only job still assesses its actual result and records it through its permitted report or handoff without inventing work or notifications.

For the full rubric, examples and machine-checkable retained assessments, see `Workflows/blogworthiness-completion.md` in My Songs or this package's `workflows/blogworthiness-completion.md`.
