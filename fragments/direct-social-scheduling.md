## Direct publication plan and continuation queue

For multi-platform video scheduling, use `Workflows/direct-social-scheduling.md`, the `direct-social-scheduling` skill and `Tools/social_schedule_plan.py` to retain one local calendar, platform-specific payloads, named-zone/UTC slots and independent native receipts. Inspect fresh queues before committing. An external scheduler's pending job is not a native upload; verify cancellation before replacing its publication responsibility. Keep one existing private draft when a native date window is closed. A pending upload/save or interrupted tab must be inspected in the native queue, never blindly resubmitted. Treat visually filled fields as unproven until platform-native validation and persisted readback succeed; wait for media processing before navigation. After a commit, reopen Scheduled/Planner and verify exact copy, duration, audience, slot and timezone, then close completed job tabs and confirm a fresh inventory. The local planner checks retained evidence; it does not publish or grant approval.
