## Beat-aligned playback and rendering

For new or materially changed musical videos, live motion and affected EPKs,
follow `Workflows/stemlab-beat-offsets-and-epk-sync.md`. Reuse saved
StemLab evidence; compare canonical and combined/fitted BPM with consensus and
individual detectors, including normal half/double time and missing detections.
Pin the reviewed source-bound fractional-pip reading into FrameRudiments' shared
clock or DanceMoves' WordPress EPK Timing metadata and exact master attachment.
Map clip/preroll/repeat time first and apply phase once. Preserve audio, LRC/CUE
and captured-recording source clocks; a fallback or poor fit is not a matched BPM.

Check the actual affected EPK's BPM/phase, canonical lyric/cue sources and every
dependent consumer during the same job. Preserve track associations, existing
appearance, phone input and accessibility. Recover missing lyric evidence only
when useful/authorised; speech masks can omit singing, so compare the maintained
full-audio route. Keep canonical words, observed anchors, the 70% threshold and
listening QA; never invent timings or structural cues. Source or approval holds
remain explicit. Plugin deployment and public metadata/content saves retain the
exact-payload/action-time gate; software publication supplies no site approval.
