## Review lyric timing and revisions

Use `Workflows/lyric-timing-review.md` for estimated onsets and artist-corrected LRCs. Use `Workflows/rendered-video-revision.md` for bounded auditions, feedback replay and proof that a picture-only splice preserves the verified footage and soundtrack. The `review-lyric-timing` and `revise-rendered-music-video` skills cover those decisions.

With a custom caption adapter, preflight a visible lyric, a clear or fully
faded frame and the next lyric entry. Return an empty bounds sequence when
the adapter paints nothing. See the [caption preflight](https://github.com/kieransimkin/WorkflowMoves/blob/main/workflows/framerudiments-rendering.md#preflight-caption-clears-and-gaps).
