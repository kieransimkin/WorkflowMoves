## Whole-picture video activation

Make every visible video picture respond to click/tap away from the central Play button, preserving accessible keyboard activation and native/provider controls. Follow `Workflows/epk-video-surface.md` and use `Tools/EPK Campaign Media/repair_epk_video_surface.py` with an explicit fresh-source profile for existing cover, native and provider players. Do not leave an invisible overlay across a playing iframe, change media/timing ownership, guess native control-strip dimensions or cancel scrolling. Verify off-centre mouse clicks and real touch taps, advancing media, pause, close/reopen, keyboard controls, source-clock parity and responsive geometry. A revealed iframe alone does not establish playback. Keep provider restrictions separate, inspect 3D hit planes where necessary and repeat the checks against actual WordPress-rendered scripts after the normal approved save.
