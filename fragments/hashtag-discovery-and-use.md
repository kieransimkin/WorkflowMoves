## Evidence-led hashtag discovery and use

For keyword/hashtag research, campaign copy, social preparation or relevant incidental evidence, follow `Workflows/hashtag-discovery-and-use.md` and `Workflows/keywordmoves-evidence-research.md`. Use KeywordMoves >=0.6.0 `hashtag-planner` with the existing source-native adapters: literal discovery, explicit exact-asset/platform review and final-copy audit. Preserve natural search phrases, hashtags, native topics/tags and video tags as different fields. Keep direct relevance, native safety/context, dates, geography, window and units; reject unrelated trends and generic reach bait, never pad a set or transfer popularity across platforms. Counts are conservative editorial ceilings, not platform limits or proven optima; Threads, Pinterest, community posts, websites and music metadata need their own native treatment. Check the guide before use, not an old copied tag block.

Merge every plausible new song/tag/platform proposal and changed evidence into the existing 21-column BOM keyword register through its guarded validator, preserving history and `proposed` state. Retain exact set/copy/asset hashes, native acceptance/schedule readback and like-for-like outcome reviews; whole-post success is not per-tag attribution. Refresh expired evidence and inspect `metadata.passed` from the copy audit. This adds no research quota, spend, account action, send, public save, post, schedule change or new notification; preserve current ownership, active-date/no-op/stop/batch rules and exact approvals. During a quiet/read-only job maintain only evidence actually encountered. Record the mandatory blogworthiness assessment in the existing report; configuration does not prove adoption or reach improvement.
