## Feed Shorts evidence into the campaign

Use [Shorts performance learning](shorts-performance-learning.md) when the plan
or claimed run includes promotional vertical video. Retain the original
retrospective and subsequent platform observations, distinguish view depth and
response from full-song listening, and carry one concrete registered opening
test into the plan/action ledger. Keep platform-specific native baselines,
one changed variable, exact source/media identity, guardrail, exposure rule
and comparable-age review owner. Unknown geometry, no distribution, sparse
samples or missing native metrics cannot establish a failed creative opening.

Only an already authorised production/publication route can expose a test.
Analytics jobs publish source-backed local report editions within their
existing cadence and batch limits; no public disclosure, extra posting,
paid analytics, duplicate jobs or automatic schedule changes are implied.
